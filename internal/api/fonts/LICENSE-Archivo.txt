WHY THIS FILE IS HERE
---------------------
archivo-latin.woff2 is compiled into the smolanalytics binary and served from
/fonts/archivo-latin.woff2 so the dashboard and the marketing site render in the
same typeface. The binary is MIT; this font is not, and the OFL requires the
notice to travel with the file. Bundling and redistribution are expressly
permitted by the OFL, including inside a larger work under a different licence,
provided the font itself is not sold on its own and this notice is retained.

Only the latin subset of the variable font is included (34KB, weights 400-800 on
one axis). No webfont CDN is contacted at runtime: a dashboard that phones a
third party for its typeface would leak every page view of a self-hosted analytics
tool to Google, which is the opposite of the reason people self-host this.
